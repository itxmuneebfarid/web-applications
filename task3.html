A Place for Learning and Discovery

The library provides students and faculty with a welcoming space for reading, research and academic growth.

Our goal is to make information easier to discover and use responsibly.

Library Resources

  • Books and reference material
  • Academic journals and periodicals
  • Digital databases and e-books
  • Quiet and collaborative study spaces

Borrowing steps

  1. Present your university ID.
  2. Confirm the borrowing period.
  3. Return the item before the due date.

Library Etiquette

Keep noise to a minimum. Treat books carefully, respect other readers and follow staff instructions.

Effective Study Tips

Choose a clear goal, silence notifications and take short breaks between focused study sessions.
