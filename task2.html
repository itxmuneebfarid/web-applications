Welcome to the Library

The university library supports teaching, learning and research by providing access to books, journals, digital resources and a quiet study environment.

Our mission: help students and faculty discover reliable information and develop lifelong learning skills.

Library Resources

Available resources

  • Textbooks and reference books
  • Research journals and periodicals
  • Digital libraries and e-books
  • Newspapers and magazines
  • Individual and group study areas

How to use the library

  1. Bring your valid university identification card.
  2. Search the catalogue for the required title.
  3. Ask library staff for assistance when needed.
  4. Return borrowed materials by the due date.

Library Rules

Maintain silence and keep mobile phones on silent mode. Handle books carefully and return them on time. Food and drinks should be kept away from reading materials.

Please follow the current campus library policies posted by the library administration.
